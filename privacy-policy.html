Privacy Policy for Gearbalance

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Gearbalance is provided by John Grey Games ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use Gearbalance and how that information is used.

Information stored on your device

The game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • the player name you enter;
  • local leaderboard scores and best distances;
  • game settings, including audio and control preferences.

This information is not uploaded by us and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its local data.

Motion sensor data

Gearbalance uses your device's motion sensors to provide tilt controls. Motion sensor readings are processed on the device and are not transmitted to us or used for advertising or tracking.

Unity Remote Config

The game uses Unity Remote Config, Unity Authentication, and Unity Services Core to download gameplay and balance settings. When these services are used, Unity may process technical information required to provide the service, including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • a unique installation or device identifier;
  • an anonymous user or player identifier;
  • limited service usage and authentication event data;
  • limited crash or diagnostic information.

This information is used for app functionality, service reliability, delivering the appropriate remote configuration, and limited service analytics. We do not use it to track you across apps or websites.

Unity acts as a service provider for these features. You can read Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Advertising, purchases, and analytics

Gearbalance does not contain third-party advertising, in-app purchases, or a separate analytics SDK. Unity Authentication may process limited service usage data for service analytics as described above. We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Technical information processed by Unity is handled according to Unity's applicable service terms, retention practices, and privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant anonymous installation or player record.

Children's privacy

Gearbalance is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal contact information through the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Security and international processing

We and our service providers use reasonable measures designed to protect information. Unity may process information in countries other than the one in which you live, subject to its contractual and legal safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email johngrey.games.support@gmail.com.
