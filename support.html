Gearbalance Support

Need help with Gearbalance? Contact us at johngrey.games.support@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Common troubleshooting

  • If audio is muted, check the Ring/Silent switch and device volume.
  • For tilt-control issues, place the device in landscape orientation before starting a run.
  • If settings appear incorrect, fully close and reopen the game.
